A plain-language description of how this preview website handles appointment enquiries.

Clinic review required. This draft must be reviewed by Dr. Shem Dental and adapted to its actual data practices, legal obligations and hosting setup before publication.

Information in appointment enquiries

The appointment form runs in your browser and does not submit to a website server. When you submit it, your email application opens with the details you entered. You choose whether to send that email. The clinic’s email provider may retain the message after it is received.

What to include

Provide only the information needed to arrange a visit. Do not use this form for urgent symptoms or send detailed medical histories, financial details or sensitive documents by email. Call the clinic if you need timely guidance.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not include analytics or advertising scripts. The website uses Google Fonts and official clinic image URLs, which may result in requests to those services when pages load. The clinic should confirm hosting, logging, cookie and analytics practices before launch.
