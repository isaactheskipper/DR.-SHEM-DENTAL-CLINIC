A clinic with a local presence

Dr. Shem Dental states that the practice was established in 2017 and has branches in Kisumu and Lang’ata, Nairobi. The clinic describes its approach as dental care tailored to patients’ needs. These details are based on the clinic’s own published information.

The Kisumu facility appears in Kenya’s Ministry of Health facility registry as Dr. Shem Rakewa Limited, a dental clinic in Kisumu Central. The registry records operational and licensed status; please check the registry directly for current details.

We want every visitor to feel comfortable asking questions. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss a concern, learn about options and make an informed decision with a dental professional.
