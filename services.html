These treatments are listed on the clinic’s official website. Availability can vary by branch; call to confirm which services are offered at your preferred location.

Every treatment begins with an assessment and a conversation. Your dental professional can explain what a procedure involves, whether it may suit you and what alternatives are available.

01 / General dentistry
01

Dental consultations & check-ups

A chance to discuss your oral health, ask questions and understand whether any further assessment or care may be helpful.

What happens

The team will ask about your concerns and may examine your teeth and gums. Your clinician will explain any suggested next steps.

May be useful when

You are due for a review, have a concern, or want to discuss your oral health. The clinician will advise what is appropriate after assessment.

Ask the team

Call your preferred branch to check appointment availability and whether any preparation is needed.

02

Professional cleaning & polishing

Cleaning and polishing are listed among the clinic’s general dental services.

What happens

The team can explain the cleaning process and whether it is suitable for you at your appointment.

Before you book

Let the team know about any sensitivity or dental concerns so they can guide you appropriately.

03

Fillings & gum care

Tooth fillings and gum-disease treatment are listed on the clinic website.

What happens

Your dentist assesses the concern and discusses care options, possible benefits and risks before treatment.

Suitability

Options depend on a clinical assessment. Ask your dentist about alternatives and the expected sequence of visits.

04

Tooth extraction

Tooth extraction is listed as a clinic service. The team can advise whether an extraction assessment is available at your branch.

What happens

Your clinician will discuss the reason for removal, what to expect and aftercare information before you decide.

Before your visit

Share relevant medical history and medicines with your dental professional. Contact the clinic for urgent guidance if your concern is time-sensitive.

02 / Cosmetic dentistry
05

Teeth whitening

Professional in-office and take-home whitening options are described on the clinic website.

What happens

A consultation helps assess suitability and explain the options, process and possible sensitivity.

Good to know

Whitening results vary and are not guaranteed. Ask about alternatives and what to expect for your individual teeth.

06

Dental veneers

The clinic lists porcelain and composite veneers for concerns such as stains, chips or tooth shape.

What happens

Your dentist can assess your teeth and discuss whether veneers or other options may suit your goals.

Before you decide

Ask about the process, tooth preparation, maintenance, risks, alternatives and the full treatment plan.

07

Smile makeovers & bonding

Smile makeovers and dental bonding are mentioned on the clinic’s services page.

What happens

The team discusses your priorities and a clinician evaluates possible options. Plans are individual; no particular result is promised.

Next step

Book a consultation to discuss the options and whether they are available at your preferred branch.

03 / Orthodontics
08

Braces & clear aligners

Metal braces, ceramic braces and clear aligners are described on the official clinic services page.

What happens

An orthodontic assessment helps determine possible options and the follow-up they require.

Discuss your options

Ask about suitability, expected treatment stages, review visits, care at home and costs before starting.

04 / Restorative dentistry
09

Crowns & bridges

Dental crowns and bridges, including zirconia crowns, appear in the clinic’s published service information.

What happens

Your dentist assesses the tooth or gap and explains suitable restoration options and the treatment stages.

Ask about

Materials, number of visits, maintenance, alternatives and what is included in a written estimate.

10

Full & partial dentures

The clinic lists full and partial dentures among its restorative options.

What happens

Your dental professional can describe the fitting process, adjustment visits and how to care for the appliance.

Availability

Please contact your preferred branch to confirm the service and arrange an assessment.

05 / Dental implants
11

Implant consultations

Dental implants are included on the clinic’s published services page as an option for missing teeth.

What happens

A consultation is needed to assess your individual situation and discuss suitable options and treatment stages.

Important to discuss

Ask about the assessment, alternatives, expected timeline, aftercare and any costs. Suitability and outcomes vary.

Additional services need confirmation. Root canal care, children’s dentistry and advanced oral or maxillofacial surgery are not presented here as available services. Ask the clinic whether these are offered, and at which branch.

Questions to bring to your consultation

How do I know which treatment I need?

A dental professional can assess your concern, explain options and discuss what may be appropriate for you.

Is every treatment available at both branches?

Branch-specific availability has not been confirmed on this website. Please call Kisumu or Lang’ata before travelling.

Will the treatment guarantee a particular result?

No result can be promised here. Your clinician can explain expected outcomes, risks and alternatives for your circumstances.
